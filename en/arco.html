Trajectory — three movements, three systems

DATA echo ATRIO

The first movement took place in first-instance chambers, facing a high-volume caseload with strong structural repetition. Before any method, the caseload had to become legible and the work measurable. I led triage, workload organization, backlog clean-up, and the digital integration of 36,000 tax enforcement cases. Volume began to be read. That is where DATA would come from.

The second movement matured with the shift from managing volume to conducting the decision flow. In 30 days, I developed ECHO, a Legal Ops methodology for operating appellate chambers: workflows, taxonomy, manuals. Predefined triage and routing for 1,192 cases. It ran for another 30 days. But it echoed: it was handed over to the chambers that succeeded us.

The third movement arose when reasoning, on meeting artificial intelligence, tried to accelerate ahead of method. In response, I designed ATRIO, a legal AI architecture: governance, audit, data protection. Implemented as the operation’s conclusive deliverables rose by 16.18 pp in five months. AI does not replace the decision: it operates on a governed path.

ATRIO — Legal Technology & AI

ATRIO – Architecture for Legal Reasoning Governance

Ability to design and implement technical solutions to legal and operational problems, with a local API in Python (FastAPI) and PostgreSQL.

Explore ATRIO →

  • Consolidates 3 mechanisms | CORPUS

    Data and corpus engineering

    Mechanisms
    • Data Ingestion & API Integration
    • Data Quality & Normalization
    • Information Extraction & Enrichment
    Problem
    Scattered, duplicated, or unstructured documents hinder the building of a reliable base for processing and use by AI.
    In ATRIO
    Document acquisition, API integration, incremental persistence, deduplication, normalization, and enrichment of legal information for governed use through CORPUS.

  • Consolidates 3 mechanisms | ATRIO / RATIO

    Decision orchestration

    Mechanisms
    • Systems Orchestration & Business Rules
    • Decision Workflow Engineering
    • Human-in-the-Loop & Quality Gates
    Problem
    Components and stages of legal production can advance without common handoff criteria, defined controls, or human validation.
    In ATRIO
    Modular integration through executable rules, a state machine, phased decision paths, and blocking controls at critical transitions, preserving the operator’s accountability.

  • Preserves 1 mechanism | CERNE

    Adversarial evaluation

    Mechanism
    • Adversarial Evaluation
    Problem
    Seemingly consistent formulations can hide weaknesses that a confirmation-oriented review fails to identify.
    In ATRIO
    CERNE subjects legal doubts to confrontation by independent arguments, with structured evaluation criteria, questions linked to the passages analyzed, and a record of dismissed doubts.

  • Consolidates 2 mechanisms | CORPUS / RATIO / CERNE / LUX

    Privacy and traceability

    Mechanisms
    • Privacy by Design & Pseudonymization
    • Traceability & Auditability
    Problem
    Personal data may circulate without adequate protection, while sources, transformations, and interventions can no longer be reconstructed.
    In ATRIO
    Protection of information at input and output, controlled pseudonymization, and preservation of the records needed to reconstruct the documentary and decision path.

    Application of language rules, structure, and intervention limits, distinguishing formal refinement from any silent change to the merits.
