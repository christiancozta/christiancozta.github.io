ORIGIN

When measuring alone was no longer enough

The system delivered lists, and from them volume was extracted: how large the caseload, how many case events, of what kind. At first instance, that guided schedules and assigned tasks; conversion into judgments was rare, and there were no opinions to track. At the Appellate Panel, the question changed: from how much was done to how much of the work turned into a decision, orders into opinions.

DATA is that retrospective exercise: measuring with a trace, knowing where each number comes from, and interpreting with grounding, knowing what it allows us to claim.

FINALIZATION RATE

Finalization = opinions ÷ orders issued × 100

The share of orders converted into opinions. An original indicator that, aligned with the CNJ’s KPI-based management (Ordinance No. 471/2025, art. 10, XI), measures the share of opinions in the total orders actually issued, distinguishing final output from intermediate output.

METHOD AND ROBUSTNESS

SOURCE

The original source is jurimetria.xlsx, a working base extracted from an internal system and used by the author to formulate the indicators. It is not official TJPR data, and the raw base is not part of the current public artifacts.

COUNT

The month is defined by the order date, when there is work by the legal advisor. Later review by another unit is not part of the indicator.

ROBUSTNESS

The raw-denominator test is shown and rejected because it includes 135 records still without an order.

PUBLIC DERIVATIVES

Public verification uses jurimetria_derivada.csv and the SHA-256 hash of the source, plus the minimal derivatives of ECHO, ATRIO, CERNE, and the tax enforcement operation (CNJ Res. 547/2024) in assets/data/lastro. The aggregated derivatives allow the published figures to be recalculated without exposing case rows or third-party data.

BASIS OF THE QUEUE | WHAT 13.2 AND 6.9 DAYS MEASURE

START POINT

Submission Date. It records arrival at the chambers, not individual assignment to the legal advisor.

END POINT

Order Date. For opinions, it is tied to the formal listing on the judgment agenda, which adds an institutional layer to the interval.

SEGREGATION

Opinions and orders without an opinion are measured separately. The composite average of 10.1 days is not published as an indicator.

LIMITATION

The interval combines the unit’s queue with working time. It is not a measure of individual performance.

30-DAY CEILING | SHARE ABOVE THE CEILING

A rule set by the author, as chief of staff, for a team of eight people (four clerks, two law students and two newcomers from March): a 30-day ceiling per docket, with new intake blocked while any case from the previous month remained open. The share of cases above the ceiling fell by 29.8 pp from January to April 2026. Without the court recess, from February to April, the drop is 5.0 pp.

LIMITS OF INTERPRETATION

VOLUME≠PERFORMANCE
PERCENTAGE≠PERCENTAGE POINT
QUEUE≠WORKING TIME
ASSOCIATION≠CAUSALITY
EDGE MONTH≠FULL MONTH
ABSENCE OF PROOF≠FALSITY

The finalization curve coincides with the adoption of the system, and coincidence is not proof. There is no individual record of use, two staff members joined the payroll in March, and April had a seasonal task force.
