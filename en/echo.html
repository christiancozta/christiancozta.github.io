01Preliminaries

ORIGIN

When there was no one to ask

When I moved to the appellate level, the first diagnosis was silence.

There was a caseload, there were deadlines, there was a panel. There was no established procedure. The available standard was the first-instance one, and it did not move up with us.

It was in that absence that the problem became clear: what was missing came before any record. There was not yet a common practice to document. It remained to define how a case came in, how it was classified, how it was assigned, and how it was written, with no tradition to draw on and no one inside who could answer.

The goal was not to create documentation alongside the work. It was to make the work itself leave a trace, a memory, and a common reference.

METHODOLOGY

What it is

A legal operations methodology built from reading routines, noise, roles, and recurring patterns.

CONTINUITY

Why it exists

To make explicit how the work is organized, where it loses information, and which criteria must outlast whoever formulated them.

COLLECTION

How it works

Through reusable materials that structure intake, classification, distribution, verification, production, and memory.

OPERATION

Where it takes shape

In a high-volume legal setting, under deadlines, decision-making authority, thematic diversity, and a need for consistency.

CAPABILITY

What it shows

That operational experience can be turned into common procedure without erasing the technical responsibility of whoever decides.

The problem was not knowing where to begin

01

A standard that did not transfer

The available logic was the first-instance one, unsuited to organizing a second-level collegiate flow.

02

A caseload without a map

Subjects, incidents, deadlines, and recurring patterns coexisted without a stable operational classification that allowed the whole to be read.

03

Workload without a fine reading

Assigning work required weighing, at the same time, complexity, urgency, thematic affinity, repetition, and the risk of bottlenecks.

04

Late quality control

Formal defects and issues verifiable at intake could consume qualified technical time before any substantive analysis.

Giving form to what had no established procedure yet

Turning the routine into usable materials, such as manuals, maps, matrices, and records, and setting, from scattered signals in the operation, common criteria not defined until then.

  • Conductor. The method organizes execution without shifting the institutional responsibility of whoever decides.
  • Conducting. Roles, inputs, assignments, and returns must operate through understandable relationships.
  • Record. Experience must return to the system as a record, guidance, and reusable criteria.
  • Regimen. Shared materials reduce unnecessary variation and make the work transferable.

Not all numbers represent the same kind of result.

The indicators show application and operational scale. They do not isolate causality and do not replace qualitative analysis.

04Authorship

ECHO was built inside the operation it seeks to organize. Its materials did not start from a ready-made model: they came from identifying concrete noise, formulating criteria, and gradually turning them into working instruments.

Its grounding lies in the collection itself. Authorship shows in the structures produced, in the organizational decisions, and in how scattered experience was turned into common procedure.
